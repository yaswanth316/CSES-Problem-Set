Wondering what causes marriages to fall apart? Let’s go-through 5 most common reasons for divorce in India! 💔

1. Extra-marital affairs or cheating on a partner is a major reason for divorces, though it’s less common compared to other issues. These affairs usually happen when there are already problems in the marriage.

2. Issues between the wife and mother-in-law often lead to divorces. This is common in joint families where the husband does not support his wife against his parents’ demands or behavior.

3. Many marriages end because the wife struggles to adjust to her new family environment. This happens when she can’t meet the expectations of her husband or in-laws, leading to conflicts and separation.

4. A lack of communication between partners is a common reason for divorces. When couples don’t talk about their problems or feelings, misunderstandings grow and can eventually break the relationship.

5. Financial issues or disagreements about money can cause a lot of stress in a marriage, often leading to divorce. Couples may fight over spending, debts, or financial support for extended family, causing serious conflicts.

1) when making decisions about divorce, prioritise the well-being and best interest of your child above all else. Consider how different arrangements will impact the stability, emotional health in relationship with both parents. 

2) have honest and open discussions with your spouse about your feelings and concerns regarding the marriage and potential impact of divorce on your child. 

3) develop a detailed coparenting plan that outlines the custody arrangements, visitation schedules, decision, making responsibilities and methods for resolving conflicts. 

4) and before making any decision consider seeking guidance from a therapist or a counsellor who specialises in family dynamics and divorce. They can help you explore your feelings, concerns and options in a supportive  and a nonjudgemental environment.

It’s important to recognize signs of insecurity in a partner without immediately categorizing them as toxic. Here are five signs that may indicate your partner is insecure rather than toxic:

❤️ Constant Need for Reassurance:
Insecure partners often seek reassurance about their worth and the relationship. They may frequently ask for validation and confirmation of your feelings for them.

❤️ Overly Jealous Behavior:
Insecurity can manifest as jealousy, but not necessarily in a controlling or toxic manner. Your partner might express concerns or feel insecure about the potential for losing you, leading to occasional bouts of jealousy.

❤️ Comparing Themselves to Others:
Insecure individuals may frequently compare themselves to others, whether it’s in terms of appearance, success, or achievements. This comparison is driven by their own feelings of inadequacy rather than an attempt to control or manipulate.

❤️ Fear of Abandonment:
An insecure partner may have a deep-seated fear of being abandoned. This fear can be expressed through clingy behavior or a heightened sensitivity to any signs that could be interpreted as rejection.

❤️ Difficulty Accepting Compliments:
Insecure individuals often struggle to accept compliments genuinely. They may downplay their achievements or dismiss positive feedback, reflecting a lack of self-confidence.

✅ It’s essential to approach these signs with empathy and open communication. Insecurity doesn’t necessarily make someone toxic; it’s a human condition that can be addressed and improved with understanding and support. If you notice these signs, consider discussing your feelings openly with your partner and encouraging them to explore personal growth and self-confidence.
